RELIQUARIES

a public-domain card game

Gameplay screenshot showing one of the player's cards in expanded view.

A collaboration between myself and Steven Lewis, Reliquaries is an online card game built with Node.js, React, TypeScript, and public domain images. It is free to play and (mostly) open source. A physical version of the game is also in the works.

Steven made the original Reliquaries game using index cards and a hand-carved rubber stamp in 2022. He wanted a card game that would involve a charging or building up mechanic––one focused on competitive efforts to build something rather than direct battle or hit points. From 2025 to the present, he has been coding this version of the game from scratch; you can find the base game code at his GitHub.

Gameplay

Each player has a fixed quantity of psyche and two decks of cards––one deck containing physical objects called relics, and one containing metaphysical entities called projections.

Your goal is to build up one or more projections to a magnificent scale while preventing other players from doing the same. To play a projection, you must first use relics to accumulate and transmit psyche from your reserve into a metaphysical space called the channel. Once channeled, psyche can be used to activate and build up projections.

Card Design

In 2026, I designed the Reliquaries cards using digital archives, GIMP, and Inkscape. All of the game’s visual elements were sourced from digital image collections like the Internet Archive and the Public Domain Review. Many, but not all of the images were digitally altered from the original; some were altered significantly or were combined from multiple sources.
